Playback dependency: pygame-ce (LGPL v2.1), separately installed from PyPI; bundled SDL libraries retain their separate license notices.
Playback decoding: imageio-ffmpeg (BSD-2-Clause wrapper), separately installed from PyPI.
Source-mode imageio-ffmpeg wheels may carry GPL FFmpeg builds with their own terms.
Windows packaging excludes that executable and compiles FFmpeg n8.0 without
GPL/nonfree options or external codecs. Its LGPLv2.1 license, exact source and
build configuration accompany the binary package. No FFmpeg binary is committed
to this source repository. Review the exact Windows wheel notices before release.
